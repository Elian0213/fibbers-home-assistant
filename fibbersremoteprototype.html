Fibbers remote — 0.7.5 design

Flat and in-system: card surfaces, 1px lines, 14px radii, accent green only where something is live. Everything sits below the wheel in use order. Live — press the sectors, drag the volume, switch devices.

What changed from the last pass

The wheel is one SVG donut with four real sectors. Last time the sectors were CSS clip-path triangles inside a round parent, so every one had a straight chord across its outer edge and the circle cut its corners off — that's the "cut-off" you saw. These are proper arc paths (A commands on an outer and inner radius), so the outer edge is the circle and the gaps are true radial channels. The hit area follows the shape exactly.

Flat, not moulded. I'd drifted into gradients, bevels and inner shadows, which is a different design language from the rest of your dashboard. Gone: every surface is card2 on a 1px line border at a 14px radius, same as your light rows and chips. The "remote" reading now comes from layout and shape alone.

Everything below the wheel, in the order you reach for it: navigate → transport → volume → channel → apps. No side flanking, no two-column shuffling inside the chassis, nothing that moves when you switch devices.

Volume is a horizontal slider, the same shape as every other slider in Fibbers, with mute as a key on the left and the percentage on the right. When the device reports no volume_level the row keeps its height and becomes a −/VOL/+ stepper, so nothing jumps when a TV sleeps.

Desktop caps the body at 320px and gives the rest to a companion panel — and drops to a single centred column when the device has nothing to put in it.
